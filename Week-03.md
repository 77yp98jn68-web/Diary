##  Process

What did we do?

We have started interviewing people who could provide valuable insights for the development of our web app. These include local residents of Ulm, employees, and passersby. Our goal is to understand different perspectives, gather relevant information, and identify the needs and expectations of potential users. The interviews will help us gain a better understanding of the topic and use the feedback we collect to guide our decisions throughout the development process.


##  Problems & Challenges

One of our main challenges was figuring out whether our interview script worked the way we had intended. Since we were already familiar with our project and had a lot of background knowledge, we often approached the questions from our own perspective. We initially overlooked the fact that other people might not know anything about the topic or understand the context behind our questions. We had to learn to put ourselves in the interviewees’ shoes and make sure our questions were clear, understandable, and accessible to everyone.
