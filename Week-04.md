##  Process

What did we do?

This week, we worked on our affinity mapping by collecting important statements from our previous interviews and sorting them into different categories. This helped us identify recurring topics, similarities, and differences between the participants' answers. We also started to get a clearer idea of what users might need from our audio guide and which aspects we should focus on in our project.

In addition, we conducted three more interviews with passersby to get their opinions on our audio guide concept. We wanted to understand what people think about using an audio guide when visiting Ulm Minster, what kind of information they would be interested in, and what could make the experience more engaging. These interviews gave us further perspectives and will help us develop our concept based on the needs and expectations of potential users.


##  Problems & Challenges

One challenge was sorting all the information from our interviews into meaningful categories during the affinity mapping. We had to figure out which statements were related to each other and which topics were important enough to focus on.

Another challenge was conducting interviews with passersby. Since we were approaching people directly, we had to explain our project and the audio guide concept clearly and quickly. We also had to make sure our questions were easy to understand and that we could get useful answers in a short amount of time.

It was also challenging to turn the feedback into concrete ideas for our audio guide. People have different expectations, so we need to decide which needs and suggestions we should focus on when developing our concept further.
